Forex Fundamentals

Learn the foundations of the foreign exchange market before moving into technical and fundamental analysis.

Lessons

Lesson 1 — What is Forex?

Introduction to the foreign exchange market, its purpose and how currencies are traded.

Lesson 2 — Currency Pairs

Understand major, minor and exotic currency pairs and how currency quotations work.

Lesson 3 — Pips

Learn what a pip is and how price movements are measured in Forex.

Lesson 4 — Lots

Understand standard, mini, micro and nano position sizes.

Lesson 5 — Spread

Learn the difference between bid and ask prices and how spreads affect trading costs.

Lesson 6 — Leverage & Margin

Understand leverage, margin requirements and the risks associated with leveraged trading.

Lesson 7 — Trading Sessions

Study the Asian, London and New York trading sessions and their characteristics.

Lesson 8 — Forex Orders

Learn market orders, limit orders, stop orders, stop-loss and take-profit.

Module Objective

By the end of this module, you should understand the basic terminology and mechanics required to begin studying Forex trading.
